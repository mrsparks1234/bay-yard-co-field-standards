## Non-negotiable site controls

1. Confirm water path, downspout discharge, grading, and subsurface flow before finishes.
2. Verify access, slopes, utility conflicts, and material staging before demolition.
3. Use written scopes for demolition, drainage, barriers, rock depths, edge restraints, and surfaces.
4. Install commercial-grade weed barriers only after root removal and grade correction.
5. Place furniture zones, fire clearance, circulation, lighting, and drainage as one layout.
6. Photo-document each gate: before, drainage, structure, finish, and furnished after.

## Why this matters

Bay Area yards fail when rain is treated as an afterthought. Covers, rock, and furniture do not fix a slope, broken downspout path, or compacted dead zone. The full sequence prevents expensive rework and creates outdoor rooms that remain usable through the wet season.
